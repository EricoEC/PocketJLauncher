## TouchController interoperability

- Public protocol reference: https://github.com/TouchController/TouchController
- Copyright (C) 2026 fifth_light; upstream LGPL-3.0-or-later.
- PocketJ's transport/queue/JNI-signature dispatcher is newly written code;
  it does not embed Air/Prisma's transport binary or AGPL implementation.
- References: `common/platform/ios/Transport.kt`, `proxy/message/*` and
  `proxy/server/ios/ios.h` under upstream's `touchcontroller` directory.
- Native/UDP compatibility must be tested with the specific installed mod.
